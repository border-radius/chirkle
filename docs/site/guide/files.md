## Attach files

Click **Attach** in the message box and choose **Upload file**. **From collection** attaches images and videos you've
saved to your [collections](collections.md).

- Up to **10 files** per message, **20 MB** each.
- Any kind of file can be sent. Images and videos show as pictures, audio files get a player, and other files show
  their name and download when clicked.
- A bar shows the upload's progress. You can write text to go with the files, or send them on their own.

A file's address can't be guessed, and only people who can read the message see it. But anyone you pass the address
on to can open the file, until the message is deleted. Deleting the message makes the address stop working.

## In the conversation

Images show as thumbnails, and videos as their first frame. A message with many shows four, then **+*n***.

The [Android app](../android/index.md) compresses photos and videos on the phone before uploading them.

## The viewer

Click a thumbnail to see the image full size, or to play the video.

- Step through the room's images and videos with the arrows, the **←** and **→** keys, or by swiping.
- **Download** saves the file.
- **Save to collection** keeps it in one of your [collections](collections.md).
- **Show in chat** goes to the message it came from.
- **Esc** or **Close** returns to the room.

## A room's media

**Media**, in the room's header, shows all the room's images and videos as a grid. Click one to open it in the viewer.
