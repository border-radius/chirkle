Chirkle's web app can be installed from the browser (it's a PWA). It then opens in its own window, has its own icon
and keeps running like any other app, named after your server.

## Install it

- **Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers:** click the install icon at the right of the address bar, or open the
  browser's menu and choose **Install** (or **Cast, save and share → Install page as app**).
- **Safari on a Mac:** **File → Add to Dock**.
- **Safari on an iPhone or iPad:** the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** use the [Android app](../android/index.md), or in Chrome, the menu, then **Add to Home screen**.

## What it does

- **It opens even offline,** to the app itself. Messages need a connection, as before.
- **It keeps notifications coming:** an installed app left open in the background gets [notifications](notifications.md)
  like an open tab. On an iPhone, it's the only way to get them.
- **It updates on its own.** When a new version of Chirkle is out, a bar says **A new version of the app is out**, with
  **Reload**.

## Light and dark

The app follows your system's light or dark mode. To choose one yourself, open [Settings](account.md) → **Appearance**:
**Auto**, **Light** or **Dark**. The choice is saved on each device.
