Collections keep the images and videos you want to find again: a moodboard to share, or a stash of reaction GIFs just
for you. Your collections, and those you follow, are listed under **Collections** in the room list.

## Make a collection

Click **New collection** in the room list, give it a name and choose whether it is **Public** (the default: anyone can see it and
subscribe) or **Private** (only you). You can have up to 50 collections, of up to 2,000 items each.

## Save to a collection

- From the chat: **Save to collection** on an image or video in a message, or in the [viewer](files.md#the-viewer).
  Pick a collection, or make a new one there.
- From your device: **Upload** on the collection's page adds images and videos of up to 20 MB each.

An item is in a collection once, however many times it's saved.

## Attach from a collection

In the message box, **Attach** → **From collection**, pick a collection and the items, and **Attach**. Nothing is
uploaded again: the message points to the file the collection already has.

## A collection's page

Click a collection in the room list to see its items. If it's yours, you can also:

- **Upload** images and videos;
- **Rename** it;
- **Make public** or **Make private**;
- remove an item, with **Remove from collection**;
- **Delete** the collection and its items. This can't be undone.

## Follow other people's collections

On someone's public collection, **Subscribe**. It then shows in your room list with your own, you can attach from it
in the same way, and its new items are counted and highlighted until you've seen them. **Unsubscribe** stops
following it.
