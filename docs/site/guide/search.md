## Search a room

**Search messages**, the magnifying glass in a room's or DM's header, opens a search bar. Type, and the messages
containing every word you typed show, newest first, 50 at a time (**Show older results** for more).

- Case doesn't matter, in any language: "привет" finds "Привет", "istanbul" finds "İstanbul", "strasse" finds
  "Straße".
- Words can come in any order and anywhere in the message.
- Click a result to go to it in the room, highlighted, however old it is. The results come back when you click the
  search field again.
- **Esc** or **×** closes the search.

Search looks at the text of messages. It doesn't search file names, link titles, or the text of forwarded messages.

Guests can search the Open Air rooms they read.

## Jump to

**Ctrl+K** (⌘K on a Mac), or **Jump to** in the app bar, opens a list of your rooms, DMs and collections.
**Ctrl+P** works too.

- With nothing typed, the most recent places come first. **Ctrl+K**, **Enter** takes you back to where you were before.
- Type part of a name to narrow the list. It matches the start of words (`ops` finds `dev-ops`) and letters in order
  (`gnrl` finds `general`), and ignores case and accents.
- Hidden and archived rooms are found when you type their name.
- **↑** and **↓** choose, **Enter** goes there, **Esc** closes.

## Go to the beginning

**Jump to beginning**, in the room's menu, goes to the first message ever written there. You can also scroll back as
far as you like: every room keeps its full history.
