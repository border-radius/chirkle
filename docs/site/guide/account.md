## Sign up and log in

**Sign up** asks for an email, a display name and a password of at least 8 characters. If the server is invite-only,
it also asks for an **Invite code**: get it from whoever runs the server.

Your email is never shown to anyone else.

## Settings

Click your name at the top of the room list, or the gear icon next to it, to open **Settings**.

### Profile

- **Profile picture:** **Change picture** takes a JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF of up to 2 MB. **Remove** goes back to your
  initials on a colour.
- **Display name:** up to 32 characters, and unique on the server.
- **Handle:** what people [mention you by](messages.md#mentions), like `@sam_rivera`. 3 to 24 letters a–z, digits or
  underscores.

### Appearance

**Auto** (follows your system), **Light** or **Dark**. Saved on this device.

**Text size:** **Small**, **Medium** or **Large**, for larger text across the app. Saved on this device.

### Notifications

See [Notifications](notifications.md).

### Password

Enter your current password, then the new one twice. Changing it logs you out on every other device.

### Session

**Log out** ends the session on this device. It also clears what Chirkle keeps in this browser to load rooms faster,
so a shared computer keeps nothing of yours.

## Forgot your password?

If the server's admin has [set up email](../admin/email.md), the login page has **Forgot password?**. Enter your
account's email, and a link to set a new password arrives. It works once, for 30 minutes, and setting the new password
logs the account out on every device.

If there's no **Forgot password?** link, ask the server's admin to reset it for you.

## Download your data

**Your data**, in Settings: **Prepare my data** puts together your profile, the messages you wrote, your rooms and
your collections, as a JSON file. On a big account it takes a while; you can leave the page and come back. When it's
ready, **Download** saves it. It stays available for 7 days.

## Delete your account

**Delete account**, in Settings, deletes your name, handle, picture, email, collections and sessions, and nobody can
sign in to the account again. This can't be undone.

Choose what happens to the messages you wrote:

- **Keep my messages, shown as by "Deleted account"**: conversations stay readable for everyone else.
- **Delete my messages too.**

Enter your password and click **Delete my account**. Your reactions and mentions of you stay, under "Deleted account".
