A direct message (DM) is a conversation between two people, private to them. Everything that works in a room works in
a DM too: replies, mentions, reactions, files, editing, search and full history.

## Start a conversation

- Click **New message** in the room list, type a name and pick the person. Or
- click someone's name on one of their messages and choose **Message**.

If you already have a conversation with them, it opens; there's only ever one per pair of people.

Your DMs are listed under **Direct messages** in the room list, most recent first, with their unread counts.

## Close a conversation

**Close conversation**, in the DM's menu, takes it out of your list. Nothing is deleted, and nothing changes for the
other person. The conversation comes back, with its whole history, as soon as they write to you again, or when you
open it yourself with **New message**.

## Who you can't message

- **Telegram users** in a [bridged room](../telegram.md): they show with a (TG) label and can't receive DMs.
- **Messages in an anonymous room**, which show the room's name instead of their author.
- **Deleted accounts.**

## People on other servers

If your server is [linked with another Chirkle server](../linking.md) and its admin allowed DMs across, you can message
people there by their full address: type `ann@b.example` in **New message**, where you would normally type a name.

## Notifications

You can be notified of every new DM, with a pop-up, a sound or both: see [Notifications](notifications.md).
