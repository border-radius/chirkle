Everything on this page works the same in rooms and DMs, and shows up live for everyone in the conversation, in every
tab and on every device.

## Write a message

Type in the box at the bottom and press **Enter** to send. **Shift+Enter** starts a new line. A message holds up to
2,000 characters; the box counts down as you near the limit.

- **Links** (http, https and www. addresses) become clickable and open in a new tab. Under the message, each link gets
  a line with the site's icon and title, if the server has [link previews](../admin/previews-and-thumbnails.md) on.
- **Drafts are kept.** What you were typing stays in each room's box, even after you reload the page. It's cleared
  when you log out.
- **Ctrl+/** (⌘/ on a Mac) anywhere in a room puts the cursor in the message box.
- **Sending is instant.** Your message shows at once and is sent in the background. If sending fails, it's marked
  **Not sent**, and you can try again or discard it.

To attach files, see [Files and media](files.md).

## Message actions

Hover over a message (or long-press it on a touch screen) to see its actions:

- **Add reaction**
- **Reply**
- **Forward**
- **Select**
- **Edit** and **Delete**, on your own messages

## Edit and delete

**Edit** opens the message in place: **Enter** saves, **Esc** cancels. An edited message is marked **(edited)**, and
hovering over that mark says when. **Up arrow** in an empty message box edits your latest message.

**Delete** asks first. A deleted message leaves a **Message deleted** placeholder in its place, so replies to it still
make sense. Its text is gone for good, and so are its files, unless someone saved them to a
[collection](collections.md).

You can only edit and delete your own messages. [Moderators](../admin/moderators-and-statistics.md) can delete anyone's,
but not edit them.

## Replies

**Reply** quotes the message above the box (**Replying to** …). Your next message is the reply. **×** or **Esc**
cancels.

- Click a reply's quote to jump to the original, however far back it is. **Back to reply** returns you.
- **Ctrl+Up** and **Ctrl+Down** in the message box move the reply to the previous or next message.
- If the original is deleted before you send, Chirkle asks whether to **Send without quote**.

## Mentions

Everyone has a **handle**, like `@sam_rivera`, made from their name when they sign up. You can change yours in
[Settings](account.md#profile).

Type **@** in the message box and pick someone from the list to mention them. A message that mentions you is
highlighted, notifies you like a reply does, and puts an **@** by the room's unread count.

### Mentions & replies

**Mentions & replies**, at the top of the room list, collects every mention of you, reply to you and reaction to your
messages, newest first. Click one to go to its message in its room.

## Reactions

**Add reaction** opens the emoji picker. A message can have as many different reactions as people like, and they
update live. Click a reaction to add yours or take it back; hover over it to see who reacted ("You, Ada and 2 others
reacted with 🎉").

## Emoji

- The **Emoji** button in the message box opens a picker with search by name.
- Type **:** and the start of a name, like `:tada`, to get suggestions as you type.

## Select, forward and delete several

**Select** starts selecting, with that message ticked. Click other messages to tick them too, up to 50 at a time. The
bar that replaces the message box then offers:

- **Forward**: pick a room or person. The messages arrive there marked **Forwarded from** their room, with their
  files. Forwarding doesn't copy anything: the forward shows the original.
- **Delete**: when every selected message is yours (or you're a moderator).
- **Cancel**.

To forward a single message, use **Forward** in its actions.

## Anonymous rooms

In a room the server's admin made [anonymous](../admin/anonymous-rooms.md), messages show the room's name instead of
their author to everyone else. You still see your own messages as yours, and can edit and delete them.
