Chirkle notifies you of new DMs, mentions of you and replies to you, and of every message in the rooms you choose. No
push service from Google, Apple or Mozilla is involved: notifications come straight from your Chirkle server.

## On the web

Turn them on in [Settings](account.md), under **Notifications**:

- **Direct messages** and **Mentions and replies**: what to be told about.
- **Show a pop-up** (a system notification with who wrote and the start of the message) and **Play a sound**: how.
  Choose either or both.

Ticking **Show a pop-up** asks for your browser's permission. If the browser blocks notifications for the site, the
settings say so; allow them in the browser's site settings.

These settings are saved on each device, so set them on every computer you use.

Good to know:

- **Notifications arrive while Chirkle is open in a tab,** even in the background or minimised. With every tab
  closed, nothing arrives; the unread counts show what you missed when you're back. Keeping Chirkle open as an
  [installed app](app.md) is the easiest way not to miss anything.
- In the tab you're looking at, there's no pop-up: only the sound, and nothing for the room you have open.
- Browsers play sounds only after you've clicked or typed in the page once since it loaded.
- On an iPhone, web notifications only work in the [installed app](app.md).

## Every message in a room

Open the room's menu and turn on **Notify of every message** to hear about everything written there, not only
mentions and replies to you. It's off by default.

## On Android

The [Android app](../android/index.md) notifies you of DMs, mentions and replies even when it's closed, with no
Google services. It checks each of your accounts' servers about every 15 minutes, so a notification can come a few
minutes after the message.

## What doesn't notify

Your own messages, deleted messages, archived rooms and hidden rooms. A closed DM opens again when the other person
writes, so it does notify.
